Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions our customers ask.

What are your laundry services?

Washing, drying and folding of clothes. Ironing and pressing are available on request.

What are your dry cleaning services?

Ideal for delicate fabrics such as silk, wool and cashmere, using a specialised process that protects the fabric.

What is your shoe laundry service?

Deep cleaning, polishing and conditioning, so shoes are clean and protected from wear and tear.

What is your pickup and delivery service?

Schedule a pickup and our team collects your laundry from home or office, then delivers it back once cleaned.

Laundry: 24–72 hours. Dry cleaning: 48–72 hours. Express service is available for an additional fee.

How do I pay?

We accept cash, cards and online payments (UPI).

Yes. If you are not completely satisfied, we will do everything we can to make it right.
